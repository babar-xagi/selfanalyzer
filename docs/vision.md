# Product vision

**One sentence:** Have a real conversation, then receive personalized coaching that shows how to speak better next time.

## Problem

English learners can spend hours in live conversations without retaining specific examples of what to improve. Generic scores are hard to act on, and progress across sessions is difficult to see.

## Target user

The initial user is an English learner who already practices speaking through online human conversations. They want feedback after the conversation, without interrupting it.

## Product goal

Record an Episoden conversation for download and replay, then analyze the user's own speech with a readable transcript and concrete English feedback.

## MVP

A Chrome extension records the selected Episoden tab's screen and audio together with the user's microphone. The complete call stays available for download and replay. A separate microphone recording can be sent to the local API for transcription and grammar feedback without attributing the partner's speech to the user.

## Success criteria

The user can finish a real conversation and identify at least one specific change to try next time. A report should show the original phrase, a correction or alternative, and a reason. Metrics must be traceable to observable speech rather than personality or emotion claims.

## Initial non-goals

Live correction, automatic capture without a browser sharing prompt, broad platform integrations, custom speech models, emotion inference, payments, social features, and a native app are outside the first MVP.
